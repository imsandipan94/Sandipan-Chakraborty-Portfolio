Miner's Gold

A steady meter and a fast cash drop in one mine.

Pilot GamesNorth Dakota, USTrue PersistenceQuick Cash
OverviewGameplay

Concept

A prospector-themed cabinet slot pairing a True Persistence collector with a Quick Cash bonus layered on top of Hold & Respin, giving players two distinct payout rhythms, a steady building meter and a faster cash-drop feature, inside one pickaxe-and-gemstone visual world built for the North Dakota market.

My role

I was the principal designer on Miner's Gold, leading the title from theme and feature ideation through documentation, quality checks and final delivery.

  • Concept. Led theme and feature ideation from the first idea to a clear design direction.
  • Design documentation. Wrote the complete design documentation that art, development and QA built from: feature specifications, game flow and UI/UX flows.
  • Production support. Worked with developers, artists and balancing specialists through production to keep the design intent intact. Made sure the title's features worked together cleanly on the cabinet.
  • Quality. Ran quality checks on builds throughout development and checked the game against the design.
  • Delivery. Followed the build through ongoing checks to final delivery.
  • Audience. Designed for the land-based cabinet player in the North Dakota market, where a game has to hold attention on a physical floor.
