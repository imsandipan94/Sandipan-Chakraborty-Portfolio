How to Play

Pearl of Fortune is a 5-reel, 3-row underwater fantasy slot with 20 fixed paylines. Set your bet, press SPIN, and land 3 or more matching symbols on an active payline, reading left to right starting from Reel 1, to win.

  • Wild substitutes for every paying symbol except the Pearl.
  • The Pearl symbol never pays on its own - it fills that reel's Collector instead (see Collectors below) and stops a payline combination from extending any further.
  • All 20 paylines are evaluated on every spin - every winning line pays.
  • Bet levels: 25 / 50 / 100 / 200 / 500 credits per spin (default 100). Starting balance: 10,000 credits.
  • Five independent Clam Collectors sit above the reels - one per reel - and collect Pearl symbols to unlock five different Bonus Features. See Collectors below for the full breakdown.
  • The Mermaid watches over every spin and can bless an active Bonus Feature with an extra reward - see Mermaid's Blessing.

Paylines

Pearl of Fortune uses 20 fixed paylines across the 5x3 grid, shown below exactly as they appear in the game.

Collectors

Every reel has its own Clam Collector. Landing one or more Pearl symbols on a reel advances that reel's own Collector by one step (multiple Pearls on the same reel in one spin still only count once). Progress is preserved across spins - a Collector never resets until it fills. Once a Collector reaches 3/3 it triggers that reel's own Bonus Feature:

Reel 1 Lucky Wheel
Reel 2 Wild Respin L
Reel 3 Free Spins
Reel 4 Wild Respin R
Reel 5 Pick Bonus

If more than one Collector fills on the same spin, every one of them still gets its turn - only one Bonus Feature plays at a time, in this order: Wild Respin › Lucky Wheel › Pick Bonus › Free Spins, with each queued feature starting immediately after the previous one ends.

Wild Respin

Triggered when the Reel 2 or Reel 4 Collector fills.

  • The entire triggering reel turns into a stacked Wild for 3 automatic free respins - no bet is deducted.
  • Every respin uses up one of the reel's 3 collected Pearls; the other reels spin normally each time.
  • If Reel 2 and Reel 4 both fill on the same spin, both reels turn Wild together.
  • The Mermaid's Blessing can strike mid-feature, instantly filling the other Wild Respin reel and giving it its own fresh 3 respins - if this happens after the first respin, the feature can run up to 4 respins in total.
  • Minimum win guarantee: 5x your bet.
Free Spins

Triggered when the Reel 3 Collector fills.

  • Awards 12 Free Spins, played on the same reels and paylines as the Base Game. Every other Collector keeps its own progress throughout.
  • The Reel 3 Collector resets and stays live during Free Spins - filling it again retriggers +12 additional spins.
  • If another Collector fills during Free Spins, that Bonus Feature plays first, then Free Spins continues right where it left off.
  • The feature ends once every Free Spin has been used.
  • Minimum win guarantee: 25x your bet.

Mermaid's Blessing - Bubble Bonus

The Mermaid can randomly bless one Free Spins session with a swarm of floating bubbles. Each prize bubble shows its reward immediately:

  • Cash: 100 - 1,000 credits
  • Multiplier: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, or 10x (applied to the win already collected so far)
  • Jackpot: Mini, Minor, Major, or Grand
  • Additional Spins: +1, +2, or +3 spins

A few extra empty bubbles float by carrying no prize. Bubbles pop one at a time to award their prize - any bubble that drifts off-screen unpopped awards nothing.

Pick Bonus

Triggered when the Reel 5 Collector fills. Reveals a 3x3 grid of 9 sea creatures.

  • You start with 3 picks. Each pick reveals one creature's hidden prize.
  • The 9 hidden prizes always include at least 1 Cash prize, 1-2 Multipliers, and 1-2 Jackpots - every remaining cell is Cash.
  • Cash: 200 - 2,500 credits  |  Multiplier: 2x, 3x, 4x, 5x, 8x, or 10x  |  Jackpot: Mini, Minor, Major, or Grand
  • The Mermaid's Blessing may award 1-3 bonus picks once your normal picks are used, instantly revealing extra cells for free.
  • Once picking ends, rewards resolve in order: Cash first, then Multipliers (each multiplying what you've won in this feature so far), then Jackpots.
  • Minimum win guarantee: 10x your bet.
Lucky Wheel

Triggered when the Reel 1 Collector fills. Three ship's wheels spin, each with 8 segments:

Bronze Wheel Cash prizes: 200, 400, 600, 1,000, 2,000, 2,500, 5,000, 5,000 credits
Silver Wheel Multipliers 2x, 5x, 8x, 10x, 15x, 20x, plus Mini & Minor Jackpot segments
Golden Wheel Major & Grand Jackpot segments, Free Spins & Pick Bonus segments, plus 4 Empty segments
  • An Empty segment on the Golden Wheel awards nothing, unless upgraded by the Mermaid's Blessing (see below).
  • The Mermaid's Blessing can nudge one wheel to a better prize within its own category - it only ever upgrades a result, never changes its category, and can turn a Golden Wheel Empty into a guaranteed prize.
  • If the Golden Wheel lands on (or is blessed into) Free Spins or Pick Bonus, that feature launches immediately after Lucky Wheel's own rewards are collected.
  • Minimum win guarantee: 20x your bet.
Mermaid's Blessing

The Mermaid can randomly bless any active Bonus Feature with a surprise extra reward:

  • Wild Respin - fills the other Wild Respin reel (2 or 4) and grants it a fresh set of respins.
  • Pick Bonus - awards 1, 2, or 3 additional picks.
  • Lucky Wheel - nudges a wheel to a better prize in the same category, or turns a Golden Wheel Empty into a guaranteed prize.
  • Free Spins - triggers the Bubble Bonus, spawning bubbles that award Cash, Multipliers, Jackpots, or Additional Spins.

Each feature rolls its own chance for a Blessing independently - it's a random bonus you might see on any given session, not a guaranteed event.

Jackpots

Four fixed jackpot tiers, each a set multiple of your current bet. Jackpot values are static (never progressive) and can only be won during the Lucky Wheel, Pick Bonus, and Free Spins (Bubble Bonus) features - never in the Base Game or Wild Respin.
