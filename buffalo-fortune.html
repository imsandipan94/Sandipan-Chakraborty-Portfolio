Buffalo Fortune

Uneven reels break the grid rhythm.

Pilot GamesNorth Dakota, USUneven ReelsHold & Respin
OverviewGameplay

Concept

An American-wildlife cabinet slot centred on an Uneven Reels structure, where reels of varying symbol heights break the standard grid rhythm and open up unconventional win shapes. Buffaloes, foxes, deer and open-plains imagery give the cabinet a distinct regional identity for the North Dakota market, while a Hold & Respin bonus rewards players for sticking with a spin.

My role

I was the principal designer across the full production of Buffalo Fortune, from the first concept through delivery on the cabinet.

  • Concept. Led theme and feature ideation from the first idea to a clear design direction.
  • Design documentation. Wrote the complete design documentation that art, development and QA built from: feature specifications, game flow and UI/UX flows.
  • Production support. Worked with developers, artists and balancing specialists through production to keep the design intent intact. Shaped the theme and the core feature from the first concept.
  • Quality. Ran quality checks on builds throughout development and checked the game against the design.
  • Delivery. Saw the game through to final delivery on the cabinet.
  • Audience. Designed for the land-based cabinet player in the North Dakota market, where a game has to hold attention on a physical floor.
