OverviewGameplay

Concept

A sand-swept Egyptian temple reskin built around a Persistence Collector mechanic, where symbols gradually fill a meter across spins toward a guaranteed Free Spins trigger. The visual direction leans into weathered stone, hieroglyphs and a sun-bleached palette, so the collector's slow build feels like uncovering a buried temple rather than watching an abstract progress bar.

My role

I was the designer on the Curse of Ra reskin for Four Leaf Gaming, directing the symbol set, visual theme, animation flow and sound design end to end.

  • Creative direction. Guided the symbol set and visual theme so the temple identity stayed consistent across every asset.
  • Animation and audio. Directed the animation flow and sound design, so the reskin felt like one finished product.
  • Documentation. Documented every stage from concept to final handoff, so the work could be reviewed and delivered cleanly.
  • Fidelity to the original. Changed what the player sees and hears while leaving the original title's gameplay untouched.
  • Delivery. Handed the finished reskin over to Four Leaf Gaming as designer and creative lead.
