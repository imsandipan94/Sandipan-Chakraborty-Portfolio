Snake and Ladders: Underwater

An underwater-themed classic with a twist of power-ups and unconventional gameplay elements.

Concept

An underwater-themed Snake and Ladders with isometric, step-mountain boards, power-ups and unconventional gameplay elements. The player starts at zero, rolls to move, and races to the finish line while sea-creature shortcuts lift them up and hazards drop them back. Numbered tiles rise to lift the character when the next tile is higher, and the first player to reach the finish wins.

My role

Principal designer: rule changes to the classic, the shortcut and hazard set, room and mode design, the daily challenge hook and flowchart, as a playable browser prototype.

Design process

Keeping the classic, changing what it is made of

The design question: what stays when you remove the snakes and ladders themselves? The answer was the tension of luck and the thrill of a shortcut, rebuilt from underwater parts.

  1. 01

    Follow the pitch deck closely

    DecisionThe demo follows the pitch deck document closely.

    WhyThe pitch deck is the design. The prototype exists to prove it as written, so changes go to the document first.

  2. 02

    Replace the classics, keep the roles

    DecisionRegular snakes and ladders are removed. Only thematic power-ups and obstacles remain, such as the Bubble Lift and Dolphin Ride up, and the Octopus, Mines and Whirlpool down.

    WhyA shortcut that is a dolphin and a hazard that is an octopus tells a story. Each keeps the original role (boost or setback), so the rules need no explaining.

    Trade-offPlayers lose the familiar picture of a snake. The theme has to make the replacement obvious at a glance.

  3. 03

    Make the board physical

    DecisionNumbered tiles are laid out like a step mountain. When the next tile is higher the current tile rises to lift the player, and when it is lower the character jumps down.

    WhyThe movement explains the rules. Seeing the tile rise tells the player they are going up without a label.

  4. 04

    Make the opening and ending count

    DecisionA 6 is needed to start and gives an extra move, which is lost if a hazard catches you. Within five tiles of the finish only an exact or lower roll moves you.

    WhyThe start gives the first roll weight, and the exact finish keeps the end tense. Losing the extra move to a hazard makes the hazards matter beyond distance.

    Trade-offAn exact-roll finish can feel frustrating, so it only applies to the last five tiles.

  5. 05

    Offer two ways to roll

    DecisionTap the dice, or tap a stack of six cards that shuffle and reveal a number.

    WhyThe same random result can be presented two ways, so the interaction can be tuned for feel without changing the odds.

  6. 06

    Respect the player's time

    DecisionThe game includes an autoplay option.

    WhyPacing is part of the design. Autoplay lets players watch the board play out when they want a break from tapping.

  7. 07

    Add one daily hook

    DecisionA Daily Challenge: a short level with coin and gem prizes on its tiles.

    WhyA board game needs a reason to open it again. A small, self-contained daily level gives that without adding systems the core game does not need.

Mechanics

Core gameplay in eight rules

  1. Waiting to startPlayers start at tile 0, waiting for their turn to roll.
  2. Rolling a 6A 6 is needed to reach tile 1 and start the journey.
  3. Extra movesA 6 grants one extra move; the extra chance is lost if eaten by a snake.
  4. Auto-runThe character runs automatically along the tiles in sequence.
  5. Per-turn progressProgress in a turn depends on the number landed.
  6. Rising tilesIf the next tile is higher, the current tile rises to lift the player.
  7. Falling tilesIf the next tile is lower, the character jumps down to it.
  8. Finish line ruleWithin 5 steps of the end, an exact or lesser number is required to progress and win.
Controls

Tap to roll, or shuffle a card

Main control

Tap the dice icon on your turn. The dice reveals a random 1–6 and the character runs automatically along the numbered tiles.

Alternative · card shuffle

Six cards, numbered 1 to 6, are stacked on the player icon. Tap to shuffle; a random card lands on top, is shown to all players, and play continues as normal.

Art & world

An underwater world

Setting

Blue backgrounds, steps, seaweed and fish, in different shades of blue throughout the palette for immersion.

Game elements

Eels, dolphins, octopus, corals, turtles; whirlpools, underwater mines and volcanoes.

Ambience

Animated fish and jellyfish move in the background; underwater plants add detail.

Shortcuts

Ladders, reimagined

Each one is a shortcut that climbs a longer or shorter distance.

ShortcutHow it works
Bubble LiftBubbles spawn from a cracked tile; the player floats with it to a higher tile.
Coral StairA stair of colorful coral; landing on its start tile lets the player climb to a higher tile.
Dolphin RideA dolphin waits on a tile; landing there carries the player to a higher tile.
Anchor ChainA hanging chain with an anchor; landing on the anchor tile lets the player climb up.
Sea TurtleWaits on a specific tile; landing there gives a shortcut ride to a higher tile.
Hazards

Snakes, reimagined

Hazards hinder progress and bring the player down to a lower position.

HazardHow it works
OctopusWaits on a tile; grabs the player with its tentacles and drops them lower.
Underwater MinesExplosives placed to detain intruders; landing on one sends the player lower.
WhirlpoolSends the player back if they land on the same tile.
SeaweedA poisonous-thorned plant; landing infects the player and forces them back.
Underwater VolcanoDormant until stepped on, then erupts and throws the player lower.
Bonus elements

Extra hazards and power-ups

Cave Monster

Snatches the player into the cave and drops them on a lower tile.

Thorns

Spike-like objects that send the player one step back.

Harpoon Grapple

Fire the harpoon gun and climb the rope to a higher tile.

Jetpack

Grab the jetpack and ride it up to a higher tile.

Daily Challenge

A short level with prizes on the tiles

How it works

Pick Daily Challenge on the menu for a short level with prizes placed on different tiles. Land on them to win as many as possible.

Prizes

Coins and gems.
