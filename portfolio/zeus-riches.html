Zeus Riches

A growing persistence meter beneath Zeus's temple.

Pilot GamesNorth Dakota, USTrue PersistenceHold & Respin
OverviewGameplay

Concept

A Greek-mythology cabinet title built around a True Persistence mechanic, where a growing meter beneath Zeus and his temple columns carries real, uncapped progress toward a Hold & Respin bonus rather than a capped or reset-prone collector. Built for the U.S. North Dakota cabinet market, the game pairs a premium mythological presentation with mechanics designed to hold attention on a physical floor.

My role

Principal designer on this title from the ground up: theme and feature ideation, the full design documentation, quality checks through development, and involvement through final delivery to keep the persistence system, paytable and bonus logic performing exactly as designed for a land-based audience.
