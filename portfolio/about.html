About

Systems first, then the fun on top.

I'm a game and system designer based in Bangalore. For the last few years I have designed slot games end to end: theme and feature ideation, feature mechanics and reward loops, documentation, QA and delivery, working with balancing specialists on pacing. I have also built original concepts to work on what slots don't let me do: action loops, one-gesture mobile design and multiplayer board games.

I write documents other people can build from, and I prototype when a document can't answer the question.
