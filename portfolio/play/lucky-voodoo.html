Lucky Voodoo

3×5 reels · 40 fixed lines · Cash on Reel · Hold & Respin · Max win 5,000× bet

PAYTABLE (total bet )

40 PAYLINES

All 40 lines are always active. Wins pay left to right from the first reel, 3 or more of a kind in a row. Only the highest win per line is paid, and wins on different lines are added together.

THE THREE VOODOO DOLLS

  • Doll feeders can land anywhere. Each one flies to its matching doll, which grows by one level (max 5).
  • Each time a feeder is collected, the Voodoo Girl may trigger Hold & Respin. The more the dolls have grown, the higher the chance.
  • The dolls that just collected, plus any doll at level 4 or higher (awake), start the feature active. Active dolls reset to level 0 afterwards. The others keep their level.

CASH ON REEL

  • Voodoo Coins land with cash values. 3 or more coins in a connected cluster (up, down, left or right) trigger Cash on Reel.
  • One doll then casts its spell. Duplicate copies coins next to the cluster, Rearrange slides a row of coins into place, and Boost adds cash to clustered coins.
  • Only coins that are part of a 3+ cluster pay. All others are dimmed.

HOLD & RESPIN

  • Coins on the reels are held. If there are fewer than 3, extra coins are added to make 3. You get 3 respins, and any new landing resets the count to 3.
  • Merge: when 2 or more coins sit side by side in a row, the Voodoo Girl merges them into the right-most coin and clears the rest.
  • Boosters: each active doll can fire on any respin, and is guaranteed to fire at least once. The Voodoo Girl can also wake a sleeping doll at any time.
    Duplicate copies coin values to empty spots. Rearrange slides a whole row left or right, which often triggers merges. Boost adds cash to random coins.
  • Jackpots: Jackpot symbols collect into their meter, then clear. A full meter pays at once: Mini 2 pips (20×), Minor 3 (100×), Major 4 (400×), Grand 5 (1,000×).
  • When no respins are left, every coin on the board is paid.
