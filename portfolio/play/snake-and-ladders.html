How to play

  1. Waiting to start — everyone begins on tile 0 (START).
  2. Rolling a 6 — you need a 6 to step onto tile 1.
  3. Extra move — any 6 grants one extra roll; a hazard on that move cancels it.
  4. Auto-run — your diver runs the tiles in order by itself.
  5. Rising tiles — if the next tile is higher, your tile rises to lift you up.
  6. Falling tiles — if the next tile is lower, you jump down to it.
  7. Finish rule — you can't overshoot. Within 5 tiles of the finish, you need the exact number or less.
  8. Badges show where a power-up (▲) or obstacle (▼) sends you — tap a tile to highlight its destination.
  9. Autoplay — tap AUTO (or press A) to let your turns roll by themselves; tap again to take control back.
  10. First to the treasure at the top wins.
