Concept

An Egyptian horror-comedy reskin wrapped around the same Cascading Win engine as Bumble Tumble, themed around a grinning, glowing-eyed mummy guarding a multiplier-driven Free Spins round. The tone leans playful rather than menacing, using bold colour and a comic mascot to make repeated cascades feel like an escalating chase rather than a slow grind.

My role

I covered the full reskin pipeline on Mummy Multiplier for Relax Gaming: directing symbol art, visual style, animation and sound against the mummy theme, and documenting the process from concept to delivery.

  • Creative direction. Guided the symbol set and visual theme so the mummy identity stayed consistent across every asset.
  • Animation and audio. Directed the animation flow and sound design, so the reskin felt like one finished product.
  • Documentation. Documented every stage from concept to final handoff, so the work could be reviewed and delivered cleanly.
  • Fidelity to the original. Changed what the player sees and hears while leaving the original title's gameplay untouched.
  • Delivery. Handed the finished reskin over to Relax Gaming as designer and creative lead.
